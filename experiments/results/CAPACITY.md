# Capacity: exactness, intervals per class, budget curve, single-box baselines

Generated by `python -m experiments.capacity.aggregate` from `results/raw-capacity`. P/R are exact (not sampled) precision/recall of the named subsumptions `A ⊑ B` (`A ≠ B`) holding in the model against the CanonicalModel entailments, over named classes satisfiable in the reference (B1, B3) or in the exact least model (B2); in B2 a class made unsatisfiable counts as subsumed by every class, in B3 a degenerate region (side or diameter ≤ 1e-3) counts as unsatisfiable. When a model predicts no pair, P is reported as 1.

## (iii) Budget curve: sound compression under a total interval budget (B2)
