# Instance-retrieval benchmark results

Generated by `evaluate.py --report` from each KB's `ground_truth.json`, `predictions_abox.json` (plain asserted-ABox lookup) and `predictions_told.json` (ABox lookup after closing class assertions under the told named-class hierarchy). Shares are answer-weighted over the queries of a KB and type that have the reference in question. Jaccard uses 1 when both sets are empty.

* **EL reference** (`el_answers`): ELK 0.4.3 on the EL projection for all non-inverse types; HermiT 1.3.8 on the EL projection for `some_inv_r_A` (C2 pipeline). Family and Father keep their C1 ground truth (HermiT on the projection for every type).
* **Full-KB reference** (`full_answers`): HermiT on the full KB, per-query watchdog timeout and a global budget per KB; queries without it are excluded from every full-KB column.
* **needs TBox**: share of reference answers not returned by plain ABox lookup. **told-missed**: share still not returned by the told-hierarchy lookup.
* **full vs EL**: over queries with both references; *exact* counts identical answer sets, *loss* is the share of full-KB answers absent from the EL reference.
